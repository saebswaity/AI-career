🎓 Graduation Project: AI Career Assistant Website

## 🔹 Project Idea

An AI-powered web application that supports job seekers in preparing for their career journey. The platform provides two main services:

* **Interactive CV Building**: Users create their CV by chatting with an AI assistant that guides them step by step.
* **Mock Interview Practice**: Users engage in a text-based interview in their chosen field and receive detailed feedback.

This tool helps users not only prepare professional CVs but also gain confidence and improve their performance in real interviews.

## 🔑 Key Features

### 1. Login & Authentication

* Users can create an account or log in with their credentials.
* Each user’s CV and interview history are saved in their profile.

### 2. Homepage

* Introduces and explains the idea of the website.
* Simple interface with two main options: **“Build CV”** and **“Mock Interview”**.

### 3. Build CV with AI (Chat)

* Users chat with the AI to provide their information (education, experience, skills, achievements).
* The AI organizes the details and produces a professional CV.
* Users can preview and download the CV in a standard format (e.g., PDF).

### 4. Mock Interview

* Users select a specific career field (e.g., Software Engineering, Marketing, Human Resources).
* The AI conducts a script-based interview with 5–10 questions.
* Users respond in text form.
* After completion, the AI provides:

  * A score (e.g., 7/10)
  * Strengths (positive points in their answers)
  * Weaknesses (areas to improve)

### 5. Dashboard

* Users can access their saved CVs.
* Review past interview results and feedback.
* Track their progress over time.
